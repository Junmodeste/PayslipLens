# Video script: PayslipLens (2:55)

Rules: 3 minutes max (only the first 3 minutes are judged), English or English subtitles, public or unlisted on YouTube, no password. Show the app working on the platform (Chrome). No third-party logos or brands on screen.

Recording: Chrome on desktop with Gemini Nano ready, 1920x1080, zoom 110%. Free tools: OBS Studio, or Windows Game Bar (Win + Alt + R). Record each scene separately, then join them.

Subtitles: upload `submission/subtitles.srt` to YouTube (YouTube Studio > Subtitles > Upload file > With timing).

**Honesty rule.** On-device extraction takes about a minute. Show the timer start, then cut, and put an on-screen note "sped up, 58 s". Never fake a result.

---

## Scene 1 (0:00 to 0:15). The problem

**On screen:** a fictitious payslip PDF (`public/samples/payslip-errors.pdf`), zoomed on the CNPS and ITS lines.

**Voice-over:**
> Every month, millions of workers in Ivory Coast get a payslip they cannot read. CNPS, ITS, CMU. Since the 2024 tax reform, mistakes are common, and almost nobody notices them.

## Scene 2 (0:15 to 0:32). The promise

**On screen:** PayslipLens home page, hero visible, top bar "On-device AI ready".

**Voice-over:**
> Uploading your payslip to an AI means sending your salary to a server. PayslipLens does the opposite. Gemini Nano, the AI built into Chrome, reads the payslip on your device. Your salary never leaves it.

## Scene 3 (0:32 to 1:20). On-device analysis

**On screen:**
1. Click **Try a sample with errors**. The progress steps show "with Gemini Nano on this device" and the timer.
2. Cut, with the overlay "sped up, 58 s".
3. Results: the badge "On this device", the figures, then the checks.
4. Hover the two warnings.
5. Change **Tax parts** from 3 to 2: the ITS check updates.

**Voice-over:**
> I drop a payslip. Firebase AI Logic runs Gemini Nano in on-device only mode, so this call cannot fall back to the cloud. Nano extracts the figures as JSON, without names or IDs.
> Then plain code checks the law, with exact math. Here PayslipLens finds two errors: the CNPS was computed on the wrong base, including the transport allowance, and the income tax ignores the family reduction for three tax parts. That is about twenty-five thousand francs too much, every month.
> I can change my family situation, and the check updates at once.

## Scene 4 (1:20 to 1:42). Explain a line, on-device

**On screen:** in the table, click **Explain** on "CNPS Retraite". The text streams in, with the badge "On this device".

**Voice-over:**
> Any line can be explained. This line contains real amounts, so the explanation is also generated by Gemini Nano, on the device, streamed as it is written.

## Scene 5 (1:42 to 2:15). Ask the cloud, anonymized

**On screen:**
1. Click the suggestion "Is my ITS correct for my family situation?".
2. The preview box opens: zoom on "Monthly taxable gross: 300,000 to 500,000 FCFA" and "Question: …".
3. Click **Send to cloud AI**. The answer appears with the badge "Cloud · anonymized".

**Voice-over:**
> For a deeper legal answer, I ask the cloud. But first, PayslipLens shows me exactly what will leave my device: a salary band, percentages, the check results, and my question with numbers masked. No name, no exact amount. Only then is it sent to Gemini in the cloud, in prefer-in-cloud mode.

## Scene 6 (2:15 to 2:40). Under the hood

**On screen:** the GitHub repository, then `src/payslip.ts` (the `ONLY_ON_DEVICE` block) and `src/assistant.ts` (the `PREFER_IN_CLOUD` block). Then the architecture table from the README.

**Voice-over:**
> Under the hood, the inference mode follows the sensitivity of the data. On-device only for the payslip, prefer cloud for anonymized questions, and cloud only with explicit consent when a browser has no Nano. App Check protects the API, Remote Config updates the tax rates without a release, and it is all open source.

## Scene 7 (2:40 to 2:55). Impact and close

**On screen:** the home page, then the URL `payslip-lens.web.app`.

**Voice-over:**
> PayslipLens helps workers check what they are owed, without giving up their privacy. Try it at payslip-lens dot web dot app.
